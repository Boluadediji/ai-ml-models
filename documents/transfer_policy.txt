PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document defines the policy, procedures and knowledge that the AI Customer Service Officer (AI CSO), frontline agents and operations teams will use to handle transfer-related issues, card issues, and product FAQs for ALAT (Wema Bank). It is intended for operational teams, the AI/NLP team, Risk, Legal, Compliance and senior management. The scope includes domestic transfers (NIP/NIBSS, RTGS, Instant Payments), internal ledger transfers, card-related complaints (debit/virtual/physical), funding reversals, recalls, FX-related transfers, cross-border messaging, and customer-facing responses via chat/phone/email.

PHILOSOPHY & GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Customer-centric: Prioritize empathy, transparency and speed. The customer's concern is handled as urgent until validated otherwise.

Secure-by-default: Never ask for or log full PAN, CVV, PINs or full identity numbers in clear text. Use masked fields and secure channels.

Evidence-first: Actions are based on transactional evidence (switch logs, core banking traces, PSP receipts).

First Contact Resolution (FCR): Maximize auto-resolution where safe to do so. Where automation is not possible, provide clear SLAs and escalation paths.

Auditability & Traceability: Every ticket/action must have an immutable identifier and recorded audit trail.

Continuous improvement: Use RCA and metrics to reduce repeat incidents.

ACCOUNT TIERS, LIMITS & REQUIREMENTS
This section describes standard retail account tiers used by ALAT and associated features, KYC, and limits. (Note: corporate accounts follow a separate, stricter onboarding with higher limits and documentary requirements.)

4.1 TIER 1 — BASIC (Low friction)

Eligibility: Phone number + minimal details. BVN may not be required for very limited access depending on product localization.

Typical Limits: Daily transfer limit: ₦20,000; Monthly cumulative: ₦50,000; Single transaction cap: ₦10,000.

Features: View balance, receive transfers, basic savings, limited wallet functionality.

KYC Requirements: Basic identity (name, phone). No document upload required.

Ideal for: Unbanked / onboarding funnel users.

Notes: Tier 1 accounts cannot perform certain functions (card issuance, large transfers, investment products).

4.2 TIER 2 — ENHANCED (Standard retail)

Eligibility: BVN required; basic identity verification completed.

Typical Limits: Daily transfer limit: ₦200,000; Monthly cumulative: ₦1,000,000; Single transaction cap: ₦200,000.

Features: Debit card ordering (virtual), peer-to-peer transfers, bill payments, improved savings goals.

KYC: BVN linkage, basic address and ID verification may be prompted.

Notes: Some services (overdraft, loans) require Tier 3 upgrade.

4.3 TIER 3 — FULL KYC (High privileges)

Eligibility: BVN validated; government-issued ID; utility bill; possible face verification.

Typical Limits: Daily transfer limit: ₦2,000,000+ (subject to credit assessment and product), single transaction cap higher at bank discretion.

Features: Physical debit card issuance, unlimited transfers within regulatory bounds, access to loan products, investment features, higher withdrawal limits.

KYC: Full documentation; periodic KYC refresh (every 1-3 years depending on risk).

Note: Tier 3 accounts are required for corporate customers, merchants, and certain retail services. Enhanced AML monitoring applies.

4.4 CORPORATE ACCOUNTS & SME VARIANTS

Require RC (Registration Certificate), company documents, authorized signatories, resolution of board where applicable. Limits and permissions are profile-driven and subject to contractual terms.

TRANSFER CATEGORIES & ISSUE TAXONOMY
The AI agent must first classify incoming transfer queries into the following taxonomy to route resolution steps and SLAs effectively.

Category A — Customer-Reported Discrepancies
A1 Wrong account credited
A2 Wrong amount transferred
A3 Duplicate transfer (double debit)

Category B — Transaction Failures & Stuck Transfers
B1 Debited but failed (auto-reversal expected)
B2 Pending beyond SLA
B3 Timeout between switch & beneficiary bank (intermittent)

Category C — Recipient-Related Issues
C1 Recipient not credited (receiving bank delay)
C2 Closed account / Dormant account credited
C3 Beneficiary returns funds / claims non-receipt

Category D — System & Infrastructure Issues
D1 Core banking downtime / API failures
D2 Network partner outage (e.g., PSP, switch)
D3 Message format / mapping errors (ISO 8583/20022 inconsistencies)

Category E — Cross-border, FX & Conversion issues
E1 FX rate disputes
E2 Correspondent bank delays
E3 Sanctions or compliance holds

Category F — Third-Party (PSP/Aggregator) Issues
F1 PSP delayed callback
F2 PSP reconciliation mismatch

TRIAGE WORKFLOW (AI + HUMAN HANDOVER)
The AI CSO performs an initial automated triage using the following steps. Where AI confidence < threshold or security risk is detected, immediate handover occurs.

T — Track & Log

Create ticket with schema in Appendix A.

Acknowledge customer immediately with ticket ID.

R — Reproduce & Diagnose

Use Transaction ID or Account/Amount/Date to search core logs, switch logs, PSP callbacks, and reconciliation feeds.

Map status code to human-friendly state using mapping table (Appendix B).

I — Investigate & Classify

Map the transaction to taxonomy and determine likely cause (e.g., "returned by beneficiary due to closed account").

A — Act & Execute Resolution

Auto-resolve where safe (e.g., automated reversal, show proof of credit, cancel pending internal transfer).

For manual steps (recall, GL correction) create downstream operational tasks with SLA tags.

G — Communicate & Follow-up

Provide periodic updates (initial + ETA + resolution). Use templated messages and ensure personalization.

E — Evaluate & Feedback

Attach RCA tags and severity. Feed back to product/engineering to fix root causes.

DETAILED RESOLUTION PATHS (EXTENSIVE)
7.1 CATEGORY A — CUSTOMER-REPORTED DISCREPANCIES
A1: Wrong Account Credited — Steps

Validate transaction details (ref, amount, timestamp) with customer.

Check settlement status: Pending / Sent / Credited / Returned.

If Pending: attempt to stop in queue (internal hold) and notify recipient bank.

If Sent & Credited externally: initiate formal recall with receiving bank and create file with proof of instruction, customer signature if required.

Set priority based on amount, customer tier, and fraud risk.

Communicate timelines and monitor recall status. Update customer every 48 hours until closed.
AI BOT SCRIPT (Friendly & Clear):
"Hello [Name]. I have created Ticket #[TICKET]. I can see the transfer of ₦[AMOUNT] was sent to [BANK/ACCOUNT]. I’ll attempt to stop or recall this transfer now. Recalls can take 1–4 weeks. I will provide updates as soon as I have them. Would you like me to email a copy of this case summary to your registered email?"

A2: Wrong Amount

Validate if the customer made an APP (authorized) mistake or there was system multiplication error.

If APP mistake: document admission and follow recall path.

If system error: treat as Priority - run automated audit to detect duplicate posting, currency misapplication or fee miscalculation. Reverse and correct GL as needed.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"Thank you. Ticket #[TICKET] has been created and prioritized. Our Payments team will investigate the amount discrepancy and provide an update within 24 hours."

A3: Duplicate Transfer (Double Debit)

Auto-detect by matching attributes (amount, timestamp proximity, payee).

If duplicate and external: initiate recall; if internal, issue immediate auto-reversal.

Conduct root cause analysis for duplicate submission (UI double-click, retry on timeout) and patch product/UI to prevent recurrence.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"I can see two similar transactions for ₦[AMOUNT]. I have initiated a duplicate check and, if confirmed, we will reverse the extra debit. Ticket #[TICKET]."

7.2 CATEGORY B — TRANSACTION FAILURES & STUCK TRANSFERS
B1: Debited but Marked Failed — Steps

Retrieve switch error code and core posting trace.

Trigger auto-reversal workflow if supported.

If auto-reversal not completed in SLA, create GL correction ticket for Finance with supporting evidence.

Communicate estimated reversal window (1–3 business days) and escalate if delayed beyond SLA.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"We initiated an auto-reversal for ₦[AMOUNT] (Ref #[REF]). Auto-reversals typically post within 1–3 business days. If not posted by [DATE], reply ESCALATE and I’ll prioritize this for you."

B2: Pending Beyond SLA

Check queue backlog, partner outages, and fraud holds.

If queue backlog: escalate to Payments Ops; if hold due to compliance/fraud: request additional KYC from customer (e.g., beneficiary identity) where allowed.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"Your transfer is pending longer than expected. I’ve escalated this to our Payments team. A specialist will update you within 4 hours. Meanwhile, did you receive any alert or SMS concerning irregular activity?"

7.3 CATEGORY C — RECIPIENT-RELATED ISSUES
C1: Recipient Non-Receipt

Provide beneficiary bank reference and advise sender to share with beneficiary bank.

Offer to open and manage trace request on behalf of sender.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"The transfer was sent to [BENEFICIARY BANK] with reference [BANK_REF]. Please provide this reference to the beneficiary to help their bank trace the funds. I can also open a trace request for you now."

C2: Closed/Dormant Account

Typically returned to sender. Monitor returns; if not returned, escalate recall.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"It appears the beneficiary account may be closed. Normally, funds are returned automatically. I will monitor and update you. Ticket #[TICKET]."

C3: Beneficiary Claims Non-Receipt

Provide proof-of-payment to sender (value date, switch reference). Encourage beneficiary to contact their bank with reference.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"We show the transfer was delivered to the beneficiary bank with reference [REF]. Please share this reference with the recipient’s bank; I can also open a ticket to assist."

7.4 CATEGORY D — SYSTEM & INFRASTRUCTURE ISSUES
D1: Core Banking/API Down

Follow Major Incident Protocol. Communicate proactively to customers via in-app banner, SMS and chatbot. Queue transactions for replay when restored.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"We’re experiencing a systems issue affecting transfers. No funds are lost. We’re working to restore services and will update you every 30 minutes."

D2: Network Partner Outage

Pause affected rails, post status notices, collaborate with partner for resolution.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"Transfers to [NETWORK] are delayed due to their outage. Would you like me to keep your transfer on hold or attempt it now (it may queue)?"

D3: Message Format / Mapping Errors

Detect ISO or 20022 field mismatches; patch mapping layer and replay messages after correction.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"We detected a technical error with the transfer instructions. Our technical team is investigating and will reprocess the transaction once the issue is resolved."

7.5 CATEGORY E — CROSS-BORDER / FX & CONVERSION ISSUES
E1: FX Rate & Charge Disputes

Show applied FX rate, timestamp and fee schedule to customer. If bank error, refund the difference and log goodwill where appropriate.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"The FX rate applied was [RATE] at [TIMESTAMP]. I have logged a review; if we applied the wrong rate, we will refund the difference and notify you."

E2: Correspondent Bank Delays / Nostro Issues

Monitor international settlement / SWIFT messages. Escalate to Correspondent Banking unit for manual trace.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"Your international payment is awaiting correspondent bank settlement. We are liaising with our correspondent bank and will update you as soon as we have confirmation."

E3: Sanctions / Compliance Holds

Strict regulatory process: freeze and escalate to Compliance. Notify customer with minimal required detail and next steps.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"Your transfer is under a compliance review. We can’t share details publicly, but a specialist from Compliance will contact you to resolve this. Please do not attempt to re-send the same payment."

7.6 CATEGORY F — THIRD-PARTY (PSP / AGGREGATOR) ISSUES
F1: PSP Callback Missing / Delayed

Reconcile PSP webhook logs; request PSP to resend callback; reprocess where safe.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"We didn’t receive confirmation from the payment provider. I’ve flagged this to the technical team and will retry processing. Ticket #[TICKET]."

F2: PSP Reconciliation Mismatch

Perform reconciliation and create finance/operations ticket. Provide customer status updates and ETA.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"There is a reconciliation discrepancy with the payment provider. We’ve opened an operations ticket and will update you within 24 hours."

EDGE CASES, SENSITIVE SCENARIOS & FRAUD RESPONSE
8.1 APP SCAMS (Authorized Push Payments)

Immediate high-priority escalation to Financial Crimes Unit (FCU).

Freeze account on customer confirmation (do not freeze automatically unless evidence suggests active fraud that endangers funds).

Collect evidence: chat transcripts, transfer refs, beneficiary details, screenshots, device fingerprint.
AI BOT SCRIPT (sensitive tone):
"I’m sorry you’re experiencing this. I’ve escalated this to our Financial Crimes team and created Ticket #[TICKET]. Please freeze your account in-app now and confirm when done. We will attempt recovery and advise on next steps."

8.2 IDENTITY MISUSE / ACCOUNT TAKEOVER

Lock account, force password reset, initiate incident investigation, advise customer to change linked credentials (email, phone).

Coordinate with Legal and Law Enforcement if required.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"We have detected suspicious activity. For your safety, we will temporarily restrict access to your account and guide you through account recovery steps. A specialist will contact you within [X] hours."

8.3 DECEASED CUSTOMER / LEGAL HOLDS

Escalate to Legal & Estate. Pause transactions, secure funds, require legal documentation for release.
AI BOT SCRIPT:
"I’m sorry for your loss. For transactions involving a deceased party, we must escalate to Legal. A specialist will contact you to guide the required documentation."

ESCALATION MATRIX & SLAs (TARGETS)
Tier 0 (AI Auto-handled) — instant to 1 hour (confirmation, simple status checks, FAQs)
Tier 1 (Agent) — 0–4 hours (auto-reversal delays, simple manual tracing)
Tier 2 (Ops Specialist) — 4–24 hours (recalls, GL adjustments)
Tier 3 (Manager / FCU / Legal) — 24 hours to ongoing (fraud, systemic incidents)
Major Incident (Exec / Incident Response) — immediate (critical outages, data breaches)

SLA examples (subject to change by operations):

Auto-reversal window: 1–3 business days

Internal recall (same bank): 24–72 hours

External recall (other bank): 1–28 business days depending on partner

Response to escalations (Tier 2): within 4 hours

Major incident customer updates: every 30 minutes

AI CSO RESPONSE TEMPLATES & TONE GUIDELINES
Tone: Empathetic, concise, professional, and reassuring. Avoid technical jargon when speaking to customers. For high-value or sensitive issues, escalate to human agents with a soft handover message.

Start: Greeting + ticket creation confirmation.

Middle: Status + action taken + ETA.

End: Next steps + offer of further help.

Template: "Hello [Name]. I’ve created Ticket #[TICKET]. I have [checked/initiated] [action]. Expected update: [ETA]. Would you like me to send this summary to your registered email?"

TRAINING THE AI: INTENTS, UTTERANCES & ANNOTATION GUIDANCE

Intent taxonomy mapped to Section 5 categories.

Minimum 50 high-quality utterances per intent initially; expand with live logs.

Label entities clearly (amount, beneficiary_name, last4, date, channel).

Provide negative examples (non-intent phrases).

Track confusion matrix weekly to refine intents.

Retrain schedule: weekly for high-volume flows, monthly for stable flows, immediate retrain after major product change.

SECURITY, PRIVACY, COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY CONTROLS

Do not request full PAN, CVV or OTP in chat. Use masked displays (**** **** **** 1234).

Retain logs per Data Retention Policy; purge per NDPR requirements.

Encryption at rest and transit; access control for logs and model training data.

Consent: For any data used for model training, obtain explicit consent or anonymize data.

AML: Unusual patterns flagged automatically; suspicious activity escalated to FCU.

CHANGE MANAGEMENT, RCA & CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

RCA tags mandatory on ticket close for incidents > Priority Threshold.

Monthly Ops + Product review: top 10 RCAs, improvement plan, owner, deadlines.

Product team to prioritize UI fixes (double-submit prevention), engineering to fix mapping issues, ops to refine reconciliation.

GOVERNANCE, MODEL REVIEW & AUDIT TRAILS
